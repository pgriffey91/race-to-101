Playoff rules
Every league sets up its playoff field a bit differently. Set how many seeds come from the primary method below, then optionally send any leftover seeds to a points-for wildcard instead.
Examples: 6 seeds, all by best record → primary method "Best record," seeds from primary = 6, wildcard off. 4 division winners plus 2 points-for wildcards → primary method "Division winners" (seed count fills automatically), wildcard on. 4 best records plus 2 points-for wildcards → "Best record," seeds from primary = 4, wildcard on.
Data from the public Sleeper API, fetched live in your browser — nothing is sent to a server. Dynasty trade values from FantasyCalc. Max PF is the highest score a roster could have posted a given week, found by optimally assigning every rostered player — bench, IR, and taxi squad included, matching how Sleeper itself factors them in — to a starting slot given the league's scoring and lineup slots. The standings show every team in one full draft pick order. Teams outside a playoff spot are ordered by total season Max PF, lowest to highest and get the earliest picks — built for leagues that award the 1st overall rookie pick to the team with the lowest potential points. Teams currently holding a playoff spot (marked with a small "Playoff" tag) pick after them, ordered by standings instead, worst seed to best. How the playoff field itself is determined is fully configurable per league under "Playoff settings," since every league sets this up differently: pick a primary method (best record, or division winners), how many seeds it fills, a tiebreaker for ties in wins (points for, or head-to-head), and optionally send any leftover seeds to a pure points-for wildcard. It defaults to that league's own Sleeper settings (best record, points-for tiebreak, no wildcard). A record with an asterisk (e.g. 3-1*) includes a projected result for the week in progress, based on live head-to-head scoring — Sleeper itself only finalizes wins/losses once a week is fully over, so this fills the gap in real time rather than waiting. Trade/drop suggestions exclude anyone in the top 12 dynasty assets at their position (or on your own pasted untouchable list) and are ranked by how much your Max PF would actually drop if you moved them — the gap between that player and the next-best option at their position on your roster.
